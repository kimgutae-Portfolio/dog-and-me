## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: mobile website dog locomotion sprite atlas. Create ONE transparent PNG sprite sheet, exactly 4 columns by 2 rows of equal rectangular cells, no separators. Top row: the SAME golden retriever in four successive anatomically coherent gentle trotting gait frames. Bottom row: the SAME grey and white Siberian husky in four successive trotting gait frames. Both dogs face RIGHT in strict side view, full body, ears and tail completely visible. Delicate watercolor and gouache storybook style, warm friendly recognizable dogs, softly textured fur, not photorealistic. Gait order contact, down, passing, up: articulated alternating forelegs/hindlegs, connected shoulder and hip rhythm; do not just shift the entire body. All frames same scale within their row, dog centered in each cell, consistent horizontal ground baseline, transparent margin around each animal. Wide landscape sheet. No text, no scenery, no shadows, no dust, no collars, no grid or checkerboard painted into the image; genuine alpha transparency.
